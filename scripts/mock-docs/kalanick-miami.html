2013 "Miami Letter" Lays Out Travis Kalanick's Rules for Uber's Company Offsite

By Johana Bhuiyan · 6 min read
The following memo was sent by then-CEO Travis Kalanick to Uber employees ahead of a 2013 company-wide trip to Miami. It has been reproduced here in part.
From: Travis Kalanick · To: All Miami offsite attendees · Subject: Some thoughts before Miami · October 22, 2013

We do not have a budget to bail anyone out of jail. Don't be that guy. #CEOlife #FML

Do not have sex with another employee UNLESS a) you have asked that person for that privilege and they have responded with an emphatic 'YES! I will have sex with you' AND b) the two (or more) of you do not work in the same chain of command.

Yes, that means that Travis will be celibate on this trip. #CEOlife

— Memo excerpts as published by Recode, 8 June 2017. Surrounding paragraphs of the original memo not reproduced in the source article. —
